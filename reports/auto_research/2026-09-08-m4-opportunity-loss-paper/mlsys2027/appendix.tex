The largest adjustment shift is $-0.054$ for Qwen3-0.6B/NuminaMath; Qwen3-1.7B/OpenMath is the only positive shift ($+0.021$). Both replicates use the identical registered 8--407 window; 408--447 is an excluded guard window.
