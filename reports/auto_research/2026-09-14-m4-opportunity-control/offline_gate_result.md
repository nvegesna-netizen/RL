# M4 opportunity-control offline gate

## Terminal decision

The frozen all-required gate is **FAIL**. No prospective policy acquisition is
justified from this policy definition.

Four conditions passed:

- all 14 authenticated acquisitions and 106,653 assignments reproduced;
- L2 and token-normalized L1 delay contrasts were positive in 14/14
  acquisitions each;
- natural control-arm stale eviction exceeded 10% of positive L1 opportunity
  mass in 14/14 acquisitions;
- deterministic OARS showed at least 10% one-step shadow L1 headroom in 14/14
  acquisitions.

The service-cost condition failed. In Llama 3.2 3B/GSM8K replicate 2, the
shadow OARS choices used 1.024966 times the valid actor tokens of the actual
selection, exceeding the frozen 1.02 ceiling. The excess is small, but the gate
was explicitly all-required and cannot be relaxed after the result.

## Natural-expiry result

Among no-added-delay control assignments, the fraction of positive L1 mass
ending in `stale_evicted` ranged from 0.5808 to 0.8621 across acquisitions, with
a mean of 0.6883. Thus the controlled delay is not creating the existence of an
expiry mechanism from nothing; substantial positive opportunity also expires
without the added five-second hold.

This remains descriptive. Control assignments share each run with delayed
assignments, and the records do not identify what an all-immediate production
policy would do under different load.

## Evidence-based repair

The failure identifies a real policy-contract defect rather than evidence
against opportunity-aware selection: ranking by L1 per token tends to reduce
aggregate service cost, but it does not *guarantee* a cost ceiling.

The appropriate next low-risk hypothesis is **baseline-budgeted OARS**:

1. Compute the batch the existing weight-FIFO policy would select from the same
   ready set.
2. Use its total valid-token count as an observable service budget.
3. Among four-group alternatives within 1.02 times that budget, maximize
   imminent-expiry L1 opportunity first and total L1 opportunity second.
4. Fall back exactly to the baseline batch if no admissible alternative exists.
5. Preserve deterministic identity tie-breaking.

The baseline batch can be computed without future information and the baseline
is always feasible, so the service ceiling holds by construction. Candidate
sets in the authenticated traces contain at most 25 groups; exact four-group
enumeration is small enough for an offline test. Runtime overhead still requires
a later microbenchmark before implementation is accepted.

This is a new policy hypothesis, not a post-result threshold change. It requires
a separately frozen repair protocol and must pass the original five scientific
conditions before shadow-mode implementation. No GPU acquisition should launch
from the present result.
